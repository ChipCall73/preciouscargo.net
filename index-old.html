Check your car

Sign in once with the same account you use for your carmaker's app. Your password goes to the carmaker, never to us, and you can disconnect anytime.

Questions, answered straight

Do I need to install anything in my car?
Not at launch. Precious Cargo talks to your car through the connection it already has with its maker. A plug-in adapter for older cars without that connection is planned for a later phase.
Why do I need the carmaker's subscription too?
Your car's location and mileage travel over the carmaker's own service (NissanConnect, HondaLink, FordPass, OnStar and similar), and the makers charge for it — typically $10–$22/month. Precious Cargo builds on that feed. If your trial of the maker's service has expired, you'd need to restart it for us to connect.
What happens when I turn on Lost / Stolen mode?
The app raises how often it checks your car's location, alerts you to any movement, and puts the car's details and last known positions in one screen you can hand to police or your insurer.
Does the back-seat reminder detect my child or pet?
No — no connected-car service reports who's in the seat, so we won't pretend otherwise. It's a prompt every time you park, escalated on hot days, so the check becomes a habit.
What do you do with my location data?
It stays yours. Paid plans carry no ads, and location data is never sold or handed to advertisers. It's used to show you your car — full stop.
